Privacy Policy

Calendar Helper is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google account data (such as calendars, sheets and files) only to read and change the owner's own data at the owner's request.

The data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To revoke access, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions.
